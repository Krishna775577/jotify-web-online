YT is a separate YouTube video player inside Jotify. Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music song link to play with the official embedded player. Your Saavn music remains unchanged.

Supported: youtube.com/watch?v=, music.youtube.com/watch?v=, youtu.be/, youtube.com/shorts/. Not every video permits embedding.

Search opens the official YouTube Music website in a new tab. Choose a track, copy its Share link, then paste it above. In-site keyword results require separate YouTube Data API credentials.
